# Loop tutorial

Learn a hard passage in about five minutes.

## 1. Load a song
Click **Open audio**, or drag a song file into the window. The waveform grows onto the track and the time display lights up.

## 2. Find your passage
Press **Space** to play. Click anywhere on the waveform to jump there. Listen for the bars that trip you up and note roughly where they start and end.

## 3. Loop it
Drag across the waveform from the start to the end of the tricky part. A yellow bar appears on the ruler and the section repeats. Drag again to adjust it. Press **L** to turn the loop off and on.

## 4. Slow it down
Drag the **Speed** slider left. At 70% the passage is easy to follow and the pitch does not change, so it still sounds like your song. Use **[** and **]** to nudge the speed by 5%.

## 5. Save it
Click **Save loop**. It joins the **Saved loops** list on the right.
- Click a saved loop to jump straight back in
- Double-click its name to rename it, then press Enter
- Click the cross to delete it

Saved loops are remembered for each song file, so they are there next time you open it.

## A practice routine that works
1. Start at 60 to 70% speed.
2. Play the loop until you get three clean runs in a row.
3. Raise the speed by 5% and repeat.
4. Finish with one run at full speed, then play the whole song.

## Shortcuts
| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Play or pause |
| L | Loop on or off |
| S | Save the current loop |
| [ and ] | Slower and faster by 5% |
| Left and Right | Skip 2 seconds |
| Home | Back to the loop start |

## Troubleshooting
- **A file will not open:** it may be damaged or an unusual format. Convert it to MP3 or WAV and try again.
- **No sound:** check your system volume and output device.
- **Saved loops disappeared:** they are stored by file name, so renaming the song file starts a fresh list.
- **Replay the intro tour:** click **Tour** at the top.
